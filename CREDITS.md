## Imagery

**All five images are procedural SVG generated for this build** —
`assets/img/weave-*.svg`. Two transposed `feTurbulence` layers (warp + weft)
over a dyed ground plus a slub layer, with GSM driving thread frequency. No
licence, no attribution, no third party. ~1.5KB each.

They are honest stand-ins and are **labelled as placeholders on the page**, not
passed off as finished. Real photography drops into the same slot with no
markup change.
